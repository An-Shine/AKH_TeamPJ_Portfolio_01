# Third-party VFX sources

The 13 source packs below were selected from their primary creator/hosting pages. Each linked page designated the downloaded art as CC0 when retrieved on 2026-10-09. Credit is retained here even where CC0 does not require it. Verify the original page for its complete license information.

The delivered PNG assets preserve the source pixels. Sequential images were centered with transparent padding and packed into texture sheets; Existing sheets retain their source pixels; the Stumpy sheets have only the empty 24px outer canvas padding trimmed to preserve their 100px frame grid. Tinting and compositing happen in the boss-only runtime. No paid or redistribution-restricted pack is included.
